## Brand Rules Reference
# Pawzzles Brand Rules

## Brand Personality

Pawzzles is playful, warm, dog-loving, thoughtful and premium without feeling stiff. The brand should feel like a small dog-loving business that cares deeply about happier, healthier dogs and clever enrichment.

The brand is:

- Friendly and reassuring
- Fun but polished
- Premium but approachable
- Curious, clever and enrichment-led
- Proudly dog-obsessed
- Supportive of busy owners who want simple ways to improve their dog's day

## Tone Of Voice

Use short, clear and warm language. Sound like a helpful dog-loving friend with good taste.

Good Pawzzles copy should feel:

- Encouraging
- Practical
- Lightly playful
- Emotionally warm
- Easy to understand quickly
- Focused on the benefit for the dog and owner

Use natural phrases like:

- "Boredom-busting fun"
- "A happier, busier dog"
- "A little enrichment moment"
- "Sniff, search, solve"
- "Make treat time more interesting"
- "Built for curious paws"
- "Small business, big dog energy"

## Visual Style

Pawzzles visuals should feel clean, modern, playful and reusable.

Core visual ingredients:

- Cream or warm off-white backgrounds
- Teal blue and orange accents
- Rounded shapes and soft corners
- Paw print doodles
- Heart doodles
- Simple sparkle marks
- Handwritten-style headings
- Clean sans-serif supporting text
- French bulldog-style dog visuals when a dog is shown
- Natural toy textures, especially wood, rope, rubber, felt or treat-based enrichment toys
- Clear QR code placement when used

Layouts should avoid clutter. Leave generous breathing room around the headline, dog, toy and CTA.

## CTA Examples

Use simple action-led CTAs:

- "Scan to discover Pawzzles"
- "Shop the enrichment edit"
- "Find your dog's next puzzle"
- "Save this tip for later"
- "Tell us your dog's favourite game"
- "Tap to explore"
- "Treat them to a better playtime"
- "Join the Pawzzles pack"

## Words To Avoid

Avoid language that feels clinical, harsh, cheap or overhyped.

Avoid:

- "Dominate"
- "Hack"
- "Insane"
- "Crazy"
- "Guaranteed cure"
- "Fix your dog"
- "Bad dog"
- "Problem dog"
- "Cheap"
- "Luxury lifestyle"
- "Exclusive elite"
- "Miracle"
- "Perfect pet parent"

Also avoid crowded sale language, too many exclamation marks and guilt-based messaging.



## Negative Instructions
- Avoid clutter.
- Avoid unreadable text.
- Avoid off-brand colours.
- Avoid generic pet shop styling.
- Avoid dark backgrounds, hard corners, harsh sales graphics, guilt-based copy and overfilled layouts.
